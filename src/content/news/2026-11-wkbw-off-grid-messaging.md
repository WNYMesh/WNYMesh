We're on the news! 7 News WKBW came out to see what we're building: a community radio network that lets neighbors send messages to each other when the power, cell service, or internet goes out.

Thank you to the 7 News team for telling this story, and to every volunteer who has built, mounted, or looked after a repeater. The network exists because people across Western New York decided to build it together.

## New here?

If the story brought you to us, welcome. Here's how to get started:

- **See how it works.** Our [network page](/network/) explains the radios and where coverage reaches today.
- **Get a radio.** Small, low-cost radios connect to the network. Our [Be Ready page](/be-ready/) covers what you need.
- **Come say hello.** Our [events](/events/) are free and beginner-friendly. No experience needed.
- **Help us grow.** Every solar repeater stretches coverage to more neighbors. [Volunteer](/get-involved/) or [donate](/donate/).

Remember: the network is a backup for staying in touch. It is not an emergency service. In an emergency, always call 911 if you can.
