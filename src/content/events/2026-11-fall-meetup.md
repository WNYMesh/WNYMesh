Join WNYMesh and the WNYMeshCore community for an evening with neighbors who are building and using Western New York's community radio network.

Whether you already run a node or are just curious about mesh radios, come say hello. Bring your radio if you have one, along with your questions and ideas.

## A Special Thank You to Heltec

Our friends at [Heltec](https://heltec.org) have donated **three T096 Companion Radios**, and we're going to be doing something very special with them at the event.

The T096 is a great daily-driver companion: its increased TX output and power-sipping nRF-based microcontroller let it run for days on a charge. Each radio comes fully equipped with:

- A Muzi Works whip antenna
- A 3000mAh battery
- A custom 3D-printed case
- A belt clip

**Come to the meetup to find out how you can take one home!**

We'll be at **Belt Line Brewery, 545 Swan St, Buffalo, NY 14204** ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Belt+Line+Brewery+545+Swan+St+Buffalo+NY+14204)). See you there!
