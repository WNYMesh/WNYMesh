Join WNYMesh and the WNYMeshCore community at Eugene V. Debs Hall for an evening of getting connected to Western New York's community radio network.

> **New registration required.** This is a separate event from the November meetup. If you registered for that one, please register again for this one.

## Agenda

- **7:00 PM: A short overview of the mesh.** What the network is, how it works, where it reaches today, and how you can be part of it.
- **After the overview: radio help and Q&A.** Bring your radio and we'll help you get it set up and on the network. Or just bring your questions; no radio or experience needed.

## What to bring

**Companion radios** (the handheld radios you pair with your phone):

- Your radio, charged up, with its antenna attached
- Your phone with the companion app installed
- A USB cable that works with your radio, in case it needs a firmware update or a charge

**Repeaters** (radios that stay put and pass messages along for everyone):

- The repeater board or enclosure, with its antenna
- Its battery or power supply, and a USB cable for flashing and configuring it
- A laptop, if you have one, to make flashing firmware and changing settings easier
- Where you plan to put it: an address or cross streets, the height and roughly what's around it. We can help you pick settings and see how it fits into the network.

**Everyone:** your questions and ideas. No radio or experience needed.

## Good to know

- **Private event.** Registration is free, and your name will be on the guest list at the door.
- **Space is limited to 45 people,** counting everyone in your group. Registration closes once we're full.
- **Cash bar.** Beer, wine and cider are available to buy. Drinks aren't included with registration.
- **No food** will be served.
