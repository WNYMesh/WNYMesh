---
title: Be Ready
description: Simple steps every Western New York household can take to stay in touch during a storm or power outage.
---

You don't need any special equipment to be better prepared. A few simple conversations and a little planning go a long way.

## 1. Make a family plan

- **Pick a meeting place** close to home, and one outside your neighborhood in case you can't get back.
- **Choose an out-of-area contact.** During a local emergency it's often easier to reach someone far away. Everyone in the family checks in with that person.
- **Write important numbers down on paper.** If your phone dies, you won't be able to look them up.
- **Know your neighbors.** Find out who nearby might need a hand, such as older neighbors, new parents, or anyone relying on medical equipment.

## 2. Keep your devices charged

- Keep a charged battery pack or two on hand, and recharge them after every use.
- A car charger can top up a phone during an outage. Never run a car or generator inside a garage or near open windows.
- Text messages often get through when calls won't. Keep messages short.

## 3. Stay informed

- Sign up for free emergency alerts from **[NY-Alert](https://alert.ny.gov)**.
- Keep a battery-powered or hand-crank radio for local news and weather.
- Follow your town and county's official emergency updates.

## 4. Build a simple kit

The essentials for a few days at home: water (about a gallon per person per day), food that doesn't need cooking, medications, flashlights, extra batteries, warm layers and blankets, and a first-aid kit. **[Ready.gov](https://www.ready.gov/kit)** has a complete checklist.

## 5. Learn about the community network

The WNYMesh community network lets people send short text messages through a chain of neighborhood radios when phones and internet aren't working. Come to an event to see it in action, or visit **[WNYMeshCore](https://wnymeshcore.org)** to learn how to join. Our guide to **[which radio to get](https://wnymeshcore.org/guides/getting-started#hardware)** walks you through the options, and our **[partners](/partners/)** carry them.
